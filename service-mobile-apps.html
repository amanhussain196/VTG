THE PAINFUL REALITY

Why Most Apps Fail Silently

90% of apps are deleted within 30 days of download. Here's what kills them — and how we prevent it.

⚡
Slow & Janky UI

Performance below 60fps feels broken to modern users. Laggy animations, slow transitions, and unresponsive touches are the #1 reason users leave and never come back.

📉
Zero-Day Crashes

A single crash loop during onboarding destroys trust permanently. Poor error handling, untested edge cases, and rushed QA result in 1-star bombs on launch day.

🎭
Broken Onboarding

Users decide whether to keep an app within the first 60 seconds. Confusing onboarding, forced sign-ups, and unclear value propositions kill retention before it starts.

🔔
Push Notification Spam

Untargeted, poorly-timed notifications train users to disable them within 24 hours. Without a smart notification strategy, your best re-engagement channel goes dark.

📦
App Review Hell

App Store rejections and Play Store policy violations delay launches by weeks. Navigating review guidelines requires experience — each rejection is revenue you're not earning.

📶
Offline Failure

Apps that break the moment connectivity drops feel fragile and unprofessional. Modern users expect seamless experiences regardless of network condition — and punish those that don't deliver.
